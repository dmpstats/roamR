# roamR: Seabird IBM For Non-Breeding Season

A package for an Individual Based Model (IBM) for simulating seabirds in
the non-breeding season in particular. This is in support of the DisNBS
project, funded by the Carbon Trust and the Offshore Renewables Joint
Industry Partnership (ORJIP)
